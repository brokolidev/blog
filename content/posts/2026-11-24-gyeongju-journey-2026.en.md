Looking at the cast lineup alone, this is a film that raises pretty high expectations.

Yet in the end, it turned out to be the worst movie I've watched all year.

It seems like the film wanted to capture the pain of those left behind and the meaning of carrying on despite everything. However, the story was painfully hard to get immersed in and completely lacked plausibility, feeling as if scenes were just haphazardly stitched together.

Ugh, what a waste of my time.
