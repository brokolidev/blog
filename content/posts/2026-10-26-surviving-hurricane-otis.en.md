On weekends, I usually look for movies that our whole family can enjoy together.

This film was ranked #1 on Netflix North America as of last week. In a Netflix landscape largely dominated by true crime and serial killer stories, you often see relatively wholesome, family-friendly titles climbing to the top of the North American charts. I suspect it is because gathering together for a cozy "movie night" as the weather turns chilly on weekends has become an established family ritual.

The movie is based on a true story, but to be completely honest, it is hard to wholeheartedly recommend setting aside dedicated time just to watch it.

My wife cannot stand cruel or scary scenes, and she is extremely cautious about exposing our baby to anything frightening. Naturally, the pool of family movies I can pick from is narrowed down considerably. You might say it is a vivid everyday example of how censorship in arts and culture severely restricts the scope of imagination...

Still, if you find yourself in a similar situation and need a film to watch with your family over the weekend, it is not a bad choice.
