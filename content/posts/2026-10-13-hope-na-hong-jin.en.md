Only after the movie ended did I find out that this film is actually a trilogy.

Feeling a bit disappointed that it's a trilogy probably means I genuinely enjoyed it and am looking forward to what comes next—while already knowing how tough the wait is going to be.

There is a lot of dark comedy woven throughout the film. Thinking back, *The Wailing* actually had plenty of those elements melted into it as well. Given the nature of the story and genre, it's hard to stay immersed unless a film is made with dead-serious conviction, yet those flashes of wit in the middle of such breathless tension kept the pacing engaging without ever feeling dull.

Plenty of questions lingered in my mind after the credits rolled, but since it's a three-part story, I'll hold my tongue for now.

I thoroughly enjoyed it—so much so that I barely felt the long runtime. I hear the overseas reception has been great recently, so I hope it receives plenty of love and that Parts 2 and 3 get greenlit and released soon.

By the way, that final scene with Zo In-sung at the end... Does Na Hong-jin happen to be a fan of Ari Aster?
