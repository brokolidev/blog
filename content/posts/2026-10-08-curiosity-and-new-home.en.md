I have a wide range of interests. Politics, economics, culture, society, sports, art—almost every field fascinates me.

When I was younger, I thought it was just youthful curiosity. But even well past the age of forty, this intellectual thirst hasn't faded. I suppose this is simply who I am, and how I will live for the rest of my life.

Over the years, I made numerous attempts to create and maintain my own blog. But self-hosting came with endless maintenance headaches and friction, and I often found myself wandering across Naver Blog, Facebook, and Threads instead.

Now, it has finally become effortless to build such an elegant home and serve it with ease. Going forward, all those social platforms will simply point here.
