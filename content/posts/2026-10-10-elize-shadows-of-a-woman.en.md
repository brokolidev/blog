![Elize: Shadows of a Woman](/img/posts/elize-shadows-of-a-woman.png)

Much like the Unabomber series before, this is yet another Netflix production overflowing with stories of murderers.

It is quite rare for me to feel that my time was completely wasted after watching a movie, but with this one, I hesitated repeatedly whether to turn it off, only to end up sitting through to the very end.

I honestly don't know what anyone is supposed to take away from a film like this.

No particular meaning or emotion resonates, and even the time spent trying to extract something worthwhile from such a dreadful story feels like a waste.

It has been a long time since I felt this way—probably not since some third-rate gangster flick whose title I cannot even recall.
